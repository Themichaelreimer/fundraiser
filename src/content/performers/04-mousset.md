name: Moorissette’s Law
description: Moorissette’s Law was formed by Greg Boettcher and a group of musicians with deep roots in Winnipeg’s music, theatre, and IT communities for Techapalooza 2026, a Canadian music-themed CancerCare fundraiser. Led by vocalist Moriah DeFord, the band delivered a hard-rocking collection of Alanis Morissette classics before regrouping to create a more acoustic take on these songs for Autumn Song. Expect familiar favourites, reimagined with a little less volume and a lot of the same attitude.
